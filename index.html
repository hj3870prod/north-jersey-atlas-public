The county line

Montclair, West Orange and Bloomfield are Essex County. Clifton is Passaic County.

Clifton's southern edge runs along Nutley and Montclair, so crossing from Montclair into Clifton means changing county, school district and tax authority in one step.

Riding in

The Montclair-Boonton Line threads Bloomfield, Glen Ridge and six Montclair stops; the Morris & Essex lines carry South Orange, Maplewood and Millburn.

Clifton sits on the Main Line toward Hoboken. West Orange has no station of its own — the nearest are in Orange and South Orange.

Why the hill towns

The First and Second Watchung ridges cut diagonally through Montclair, Verona, West Orange and Millburn.

The ridge separates the hill side of these towns from the flatter, denser eastern half — and it is why Bloomfield and Clifton, both off the ridge, grew as valley towns.

About Middlesex

Middlesex is on the map at your request, but it is almost always counted as Central Jersey, not North.

The usual northern eight are Bergen, Passaic, Sussex, Warren, Morris, Essex, Hudson and Union; Somerset and Hunterdon get folded in about as often as Middlesex does.
